Overview

This privacy notice explains how the SynthHair research project processes personal data. Our research focuses on computer vision, specifically developing machine learning models capable of hair matting (separating facial hair from backgrounds) and predicting hair properties such as depth, growth direction, and hair type (beard, scalp hair, eyebrows, eyelashes, mustache).

To conduct this research, we use standard, pre-existing, third-party academic datasets of images. Because these datasets contain thousands of images originally sourced from the internet, they may contain personal information (such as faces).

Data Controller and Lawful Basis

University of Oxford is the Data Controller with respect to your personal data and determines how it is used in this research. The University processes your personal data for the purpose of the scientific research outlined above. Research is a task performed in the public interest.

How the Data is Processed

The data in this project is used strictly for non-commercial scientific research.

  1. Synthetic Data Generation: We extract 2D/3D facial expression marker points (landmarks) from approximately 1,000 images in the AffectNet dataset. These mathematical points are non-identifiable and cannot be mapped back to the original person. We use these marker points to animate digital “MetaHumans” to create a 100% synthetic dataset of faces and hair, which is then composed onto generic background images.
  2. Model Evaluation: We evaluate the performance of our models by testing them on existing, publicly available datasets containing real human faces (see the list below). We also train internal evaluation models on these real datasets purely to compare the performance differences between training on real versus synthetic data.

The algorithms in this project are not designed to identify individuals or extract biometric identification data (such as facial recognition).

Why the Data is Processed

Modern machine learning techniques require standardized images to learn and to benchmark performance. Using these pre-existing third-party datasets is the only way to measure our algorithm’s accuracy, compare it against previous scientific work, and demonstrate measurable progress in scientific publications.

Who is Using the Data

The data is used exclusively by researchers affiliated with the SynthHair project at the University of Oxford. The raw data is stored securely on local institutional servers and access is strictly restricted to affiliated researchers. No raw personal data is transferred internationally.

What Data is Shared

We do not share, publish, or distribute the raw, original datasets containing personal data, nor do we release any of the internal evaluation models trained on real data.

To support open science, we will publicly release:

  • The fully synthetic dataset generated by the project (which contains no personal data).
  • The final machine learning model trained on the synthetic dataset.
  • Aggregate statistics and performance metrics in scientific papers.

Data Retention

We only keep personal data for as long as necessary:

  • Publicly Available Datasets (Real Images): Kept only as long as necessary for the research and for up to three years to support verification and reproducibility of the research results. Where possible, datasets will be deleted and re-downloaded from their original sources if needed; otherwise, they may be retained in secure, restricted-access storage for a period of up to 3 years after the completion of the project to ensure the reproducibility of experiments.
  • Intermediate Data (Facial Landmarks): Retained only during the dataset creation phase and permanently deleted once the synthetic dataset is rendered.
  • Synthetic Dataset & Synthetic Models: Retained long-term (10+ years) for research reproducibility, as these do not contain personal data.

Your Rights and How to Request Erasure

Under the UK GDPR, you have rights regarding your personal data, including the right to be informed, access, rectification, erasure, restrict processing, and to object. If you think your personal information is used in the project and wish it to be erased, please contact the University’s central Information Compliance Team:

  • Email Address: information.compliance@admin.ox.ac.uk
  • Email Subject: SynthHair Data Erasure Request - Department of Engineering (TVG)
  • Clearly specify which image(s) you would like us to erase.

After verifying your request, we will delete the relevant image(s) or video(s) from our system and stop using them in the research.

Note: Because we use existing third-party datasets and do not hold names or metadata linked to the images, we may require a reference image or specific file name from you to locate and delete your data. Furthermore, while we will delete your data from our local project servers, we cannot remove it from the original third-party source dataset.

List of Datasets Used in this Project

This project utilizes the following pre-existing datasets:

  • AffectNet (used for non-identifiable facial expression extraction)
  • Backgrounds of Background Matting V2 (for background compositions)
  • CelebA
  • CelebAMask-HQ
  • Clothing Dataset (for background compositions)
  • DIS5K
  • Face Synthetics
  • Figaro-1k
  • HHM2K
  • HiSa
  • K-Hairstyle
  • P3M-10k
  • PPM-100
  • ThinObject-5K
